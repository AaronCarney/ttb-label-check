# Mandatory label elements: wine, distilled spirits, malt beverages

Sources: 27 CFR part 4 (wine), part 5 (distilled spirits), part 7 (malt beverages), read from eCFR
<https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/part-4>, <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/part-5>,
<https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/part-7> (text taken through the eCFR API,
`https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-09-11/title-27.xml?part=N`). eCFR shows Title 27
current as of 2026-09-11. Read 2026-09-15. Summarised faithfully; phrases in quotation marks are
the regulation's words. Country-of-origin marking is CBP's rule, 19 CFR 134.45
(<https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-134.45>, current as of 2026-09-11).

The health warning, required on all three, is in [health-warning.md](health-warning.md).

## Which elements, and where they go

| Element | Wine (part 4) | Distilled spirits (part 5) | Malt beverages (part 7) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand name | Brand label, 4.32(a)(1) | Same field of vision, 5.63(a)(1) | Any label, 7.63(a)(1) |
| Class/type | Brand label, 4.32(a)(2) | Same field of vision, 5.63(a)(2) | Any label, 7.63(a)(2) |
| Alcohol content | Any label, 4.32(b)(3). Required above 14% ABV; at or below 14% optional if "table" or "light" wine is on the brand label, 4.36(a) | Required; same field of vision, 5.63(a)(3) | Required only if alcohol comes from added nonbeverage flavors or ingredients (not hops extract), 7.63(a)(3); otherwise optional unless State law prohibits it, 7.65(a) |
| Name and address | Any label, 4.32(b)(1) | Anywhere on the container, 5.63(b)(1) | Any label; may be blown or embossed, 7.63(a)(4) |
| Net contents | Any label, 4.32(b)(2); front label if not a metric standard of fill; may be permanently marked in the bottle, 4.37(c) | Anywhere; may be blown or embossed, 5.63(b)(2) | Any label; may be blown or embossed, 7.63(a)(5) |
| Country of origin (imports) | Cross-reference to CBP, 19 CFR parts 102 and 134: 4.35(e) | Same, 5.69 | Same, 7.69 |

- **Field of vision** is a spirits-only rule: brand name, class/type and alcohol content must sit
  "within the same field of vision (which means a single side of a container (for a cylindrical
  container, a side is 40 percent of the circumference) where all of the pieces of information can
  be viewed simultaneously without the need to turn the container)", 5.63(a). Also same field of
  vision: a proof statement used with the mandatory ABV, 5.65(b)(1)(i); U.S. customary or centiliter
  equivalents next to spirits' metric net contents, 5.70(a); a type shown with its class, 5.141(c);
  metric measures next to malt beverages' U.S. units, 7.70(c).
- **Brand label** (wine) is "The label carrying, in the usual distinctive design, the brand name of
  the wine", 4.10.
- **No brand name:** the name of the bottler, distiller or importer is treated as the brand name,
  4.33(a), 5.64(a), 7.64(a).
- **"Brand" qualifier:** a brand name that would mislead may be qualified with the word "brand",
  4.33(b), 5.64(b), 7.64(b).

## Alcohol content

| | Wine, 4.36 | Distilled spirits, 5.65 | Malt beverages, 7.65 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Formats | "Alcohol __ % by volume," or "Alcohol __ % to __ % by volume," "or similar appropriate phrase" | "Alcohol ____ percent by volume"; "____ percent alcohol by volume"; "Alcohol by volume ____ percent." | "Alcohol percent by volume"; "percent alcohol by volume"; "Alcohol by volume: percent." Stated to the nearest 0.1 at 0.5% or more |
| Abbreviations | "alc." (alc) and "vol." (vol) | "alc", "%", "/" for "by", "vol"; any word or symbol may be in parentheses; periods optional | Same as spirits |
| Examples given | none | "40% alc/vol"; "Alc. 40 percent by vol."; "Alc 40% by vol"; "40% Alcohol by Volume." | "4.2% alc/vol"; "Alc. 4.0 percent by vol."; "Alc 4% by vol"; "5.9% Alcohol by Volume." |
| Tolerance, label vs actual content | ±1 point above 14%, ±1.5 points at or below 14%; a range may span at most 2 or 3 points; never across a class or tax-grade limit, 4.36(c) | ±0.3 percentage points, 5.65(c) | ±0.3 points at 0.5% or more, never below 0.5%, 7.65(c). "Low/reduced alcohol" under 2.5%; "non-alcoholic" needs "contains less than 0.5 percent (or .5%) alcohol by volume" adjacent; "alcohol free" means none, 7.65(d)–(f) |

- **Proof** (spirits only, optional): "The ethyl alcohol content of a liquid at 60 degrees
  Fahrenheit, stated as twice the percentage of ethyl alcohol by volume", 5.1. It may appear only
  alongside the ABV statement, 5.65(b)(1)(i).
- Solid-fruit spirits state "Bottled at ____ percent alcohol by volume.", 5.65(a).
- The tolerances compare the label with the liquid in the bottle, not with the application form.

## Net contents

- **Wine, 4.37 and 4.72.** Stated "in the same manner and form as set forth in the standard of
  fill". Otherwise: over 1 L in liters to the nearest hundredth; under 1 L in milliliters "(ml)".
  A U.S. equivalent may follow, in fl. oz. to the nearest tenth, e.g. "700 ml (23.7 fl. oz.)"; for
  750 mL the table gives 25.4 fl. oz. Standards of fill: 3, 2.25, 1.8, 1.5 and 1 L; 750, 720, 700,
  620, 600, 568, 550, 500, 473, 375, 360, 355, 330, 300, 250, 200, 187, 180, 100 and 50 mL; 4 L and
  up in even liters.
- **Distilled spirits, 5.70 and 5.203.** "liter" may be spelled "litre" or abbreviated "L";
  "milliliters" may be abbreviated "ml.," "mL.," or "ML." U.S. customary units and centiliters are
  optional and must share the field of vision with the metric statement. Standards of fill: 3.75,
  3, 2, 1.8, 1.75, 1.5 and 1.00 L; 945, 900, 750, 720, 710, 700, 570, 500, 475, 375, 355, 350, 331,
  250, 200, 187, 100 and 50 mL.
- **Malt beverages, 7.70.** U.S. customary units are required: under a pint in fluid ounces or
  fractions of a pint; exactly a pint, quart or gallon "so stated"; larger sizes in quarts, pints,
  fluid ounces or gallons; fractions in lowest terms. Metric "in addition to, but not in lieu of",
  in the same field of vision. No standards of fill.
- Wine and spirits allow only good-practice filling, container and evaporation discrepancies as
  tolerance, with no numeric figure, 4.37(d), 5.70(b). Part 7 states no net-contents tolerance.

## Name and address

- **Wine, 4.35.** American wine: "bottled by" or "packed by" + name + address of the place of
  bottling. Imports: "imported by" or similar + importer's name + U.S. principal place of business.
  The address is the post office address on the basic permit; "the street address may be omitted",
  4.35(c). A trade name must be "identical with a name appearing on the basic permit", 4.35(d).
- **Distilled spirits, 5.66–5.68.** The name follows a phrase naming the function ("bottled by",
  "distilled by", "produced by", "imported by" and so on). The address is city and State,
  "consistent with the information on the basic permit"; street, ZIP, phone and website are
  optional; "The postal abbreviation of the State name may be used; for example, California may be
  abbreviated as CA", 5.66(d)(1), 5.68(b)(2).
- **Malt beverages, 7.66–7.68.** Domestic: name and address of the bottler; the function phrase is
  optional, 7.66(d). Imports: "imported by" or similar. Address is city and State; the same
  optional extras and postal abbreviation, 7.66(c), 7.68(b)(2).

## Country of origin (CBP, 19 CFR 134.45)

The marking must "include the full English name of the country of origin", 134.45(a)(1).
"Abbreviations which unmistakably indicate the name of a country, such as 'Gt. Britain'" and
"Variant spellings which clearly indicate the English name of the country of origin, such as
'Brasil' for 'Brazil' and 'Italie' for 'Italy,'" are acceptable, 134.45(b). The adjectival form is
acceptable unless it names a kind of product ("English walnuts"), 134.45(c).

## Presentation rules the checks depend on

- **Capitals:** spirits and malt mandatory information "may appear in all capital letters, in all
  lower case letters, or in mixed-case", 5.52(d), 7.52(d). Part 4 has no equivalent clause.
- **Separate and apart:** spirits and malt mandatory information, except brand names, "must be
  separate and apart from any additional information"; brief optional phrases may join the
  class/type ("premium vodka") or the name and address, 5.52(b), 7.52(b).
- **Designation words:** spirits: "All words in a designation must be similarly conspicuous and
  must appear together", 5.141(d); "whisky" may be spelled "whisky" or "whiskey", 5.143(b). Wine:
  all parts of the designation "in direct conjunction and in lettering substantially of the same
  size and kind", 4.34(a).
- **Minimum type height:** wine 2 mm over 187 mL, 1 mm at or under; the wine alcohol statement
  1–3 mm on containers of 5 L or less, 4.38(b). Spirits 2 mm over 200 mL, 1 mm at or under, 5.53.
  Malt 2 mm over a half pint, 1 mm at or under; alcohol statement at most 3 mm (40 fl oz or less)
  or 4 mm (over 40 fl oz), 7.53.
- **Legibility:** "readily legible" on a contrasting background, 4.38(a), 5.52(a) and (c), 7.52(a)
  and (c).
