Bulk label upload

Pick multiple PNG or JPEG files. Each becomes one label in a single batch; the streaming results page opens once the worker spawns.

Two photographs of one label? Name them anything-front.jpg and anything-back.jpg and they are checked together as one label. That matters because the government warning is usually printed on the back, so a front on its own fails the warning check and a back on its own has no brand or class on it. Any other filename is one label on its own.

Don’t have label images? Download a 10-label sample pulled from the TTB Public COLA Registry (CC0). Unzip and drop into the file picker below to exercise the full pipeline. Its files are already named that way, so the labels that have two faces arrive as two faces.

A bulk upload carries images and no applications, so nothing is compared against one. The beverage type still decides which rules apply: a wine label checked against the spirits rules fires none of the wine rules. To compare a label against its application, submit it on the single-label page.
